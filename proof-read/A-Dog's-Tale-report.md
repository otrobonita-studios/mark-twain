## DETAILED FINDINGS

### SUBSTANTIVE VARIANTS (Meaning-Affecting)

#### 1. **CRITICAL SUBSTITUTION — Page break in original text**
**Location:** Chapter IV (post-rescue narrative), regarding the puppy's death
**Working Copy:** "...and say I risked my life to **save** the baby's..."
**1904 Original:** "...and say I risked my life to **say** the baby's..."

**Analysis:** This is a clear OCR or transcription error. The phrase should read "risked my life to **say** the baby's" — an idiom meaning "to protect the baby's life." The working copy's substitution of "save" for "say" creates redundancy with the surrounding narrative ("risked my life to save the baby") and obscures the archaic phrasing characteristic of Twain's period voice. This appears to be a correction attempt that actually introduces an error, or an OCR misread (the original typeface may have rendered "say" ambiguously).

**Classification:** Substitution/Word Error | Semantic impact: Minor but notable
