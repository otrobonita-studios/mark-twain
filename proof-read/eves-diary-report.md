## Notes on scope

**Emphasis fidelity is the dominant issue in this text.** Twain used italics heavily throughout Eve's Diary — for Eve's coined maxims, for emphatic pronouns in the grammar discussion, for sudden discoveries (FIRE, SMOKE, FLAMES, FEAR), and for Adam's closing epitaph. Every one of these has been converted to ALL CAPS by the ingest pipeline. A restoration pass must go page-by-page through the 1906 scan to recover every italicised word and restore it properly.

The editorial note ("first reworked version of many to come") embedded in the Traditional Read view should be removed or placed outside the text column; it misleads readers about what they are reading.
