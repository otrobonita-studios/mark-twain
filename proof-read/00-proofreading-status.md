## Notes on scope

- **Novels & major works** are the highest priority; these are what most readers use.  
- **Encyclopedia entries** that appear twice (with and without `-Clemens-Samuel-Langhorne` suffix) are the same print source — one page shows the full volume context, the other shows just the Twain entry. Only the entry page needs proofreading against the original volume.  
- **Newspaper articles** will be checked against the digitised originals on Chronicling America / newspaper archives where available.  
- Reports are collation-based per the **restored-text-proofreader** skill: flag only, no silent fixes.
