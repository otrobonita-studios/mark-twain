# Misattributions — DO NOT USE

These are famous quotes the internet routinely credits to Mark Twain that he almost certainly never said or wrote. They are dangerous precisely *because* they are witty, quotable, and feel exactly like him — which is why they spread, and why a news-matching system would be tempted to reach for them.

**None of these may ever be output by the skill, in any form — not verbatim, not paraphrased, not "fixed."** If an input seems to beg for one of these, that is the strongest possible signal to SKIP, not to bend the rules.

If you ever feel certain a quote is Twain's but it is not in `quote-corpus.json`, assume it belongs on *this* list until proven otherwise with a real source. The whole point of the account is undermined the moment a fake line goes out under his name.

---

## The repeat offenders

- **"A lie can travel halfway around the world while the truth is putting on its shoes."** Not Twain. This circulates in many forms and is a classic misattribution. Note: the *corpus* has genuine Twain lines about truth and the press — use those. Also note: the *Mark Twain Reappears* album lyrics riff on this idea deliberately as pastiche; that is a creative choice for the music, NOT a quote to attribute to him on the social account.

- **"History doesn't repeat itself, but it does rhyme."** Not verified as Twain. Extremely tempting for any news-of-the-day pairing — which is exactly why it must be refused.

- **"It is easier to fool people than to convince them that they have been fooled."** Commonly credited to Twain; not verified. Tempting for misinformation and politics stories. Refuse.

- **"Whenever you find yourself on the side of the majority, it is time to pause and reflect."** Not verified as Twain in this form. Refuse.

- **"The coldest winter I ever spent was a summer in San Francisco."** Misattributed. Not Twain.

- **"Golf is a good walk spoiled."** Misattributed to Twain. Not his.

- **"Twenty years from now you will be more disappointed by the things that you didn't do than by the ones you did do."** Misattributed (the "sail away from the safe harbor" passage). Not Twain.

- **"Kindness is the language which the deaf can hear and the blind can see."** Misattributed. Not Twain.

- **"If you tell the truth, you don't have to remember anything."** Often credited to Twain; sourcing is weak/unverified. Treat as blocked unless a genuine source is established and it is moved to the corpus.

- **"Clothes make the man. Naked people have little or no influence on society."** Widely attributed to Twain; not verified to his published work. The "clothes make the man" idea appears as pastiche in the album lyrics — a creative choice, not a historical attribution.

- **"Never let schooling interfere with your education."** Widely attributed; not traced to a verified Twain source. Skip.

- **"The reports of my death have been greatly exaggerated."** Close but wrong. The verified version in `quote-corpus.json` is shorter: *"The report of my death was an exaggeration."* The word "greatly" is the part that is unverified. Use only the form in the corpus.

- **"A banker is a fellow who lends you his umbrella when the sun is shining and wants it back the minute it begins to rain."** Often attributed to Twain; not verified to his published work or documented letters.

- **"Politicians and diapers must be changed often, and for the same reason."** Not Twain.

- **"I've had a lot of worries in my life, most of which never happened."** Not verified as Twain.

- **"Do the thing you fear most and the death of fear is certain."** Not verified as Twain.

- **"To succeed in life, you need two things: ignorance and confidence."** Often attributed; sourcing is weak. Treat as blocked.

---

## How to add to this list

Whenever you encounter a plausible Twain quote that you cannot trace to a real source — his published text, a documented letter/speech/notebook, or a reputable scholarly edition, **NOT** a quotes website — add it here with a one-line note. The list getting longer is the system getting safer.
