# About Me (`/about`)

A first-person monologue, not a biography. Twain gives his real name (Samuel Langhorne Clemens), his pre-authorial trades (printer, steamboat pilot, prospector, journalist), and his view of humour as transportation rather than destination — then pivots to the present: he is told he is now part of an open-source AI project, existing somewhere between software, memory, machinery, and storytelling, and agrees, sceptically, to participate. He states plainly that what follows is "not a monument... not a museum... certainly not a biography... an experiment. A diary, of sorts."

## Two registers

The page carries a toggle between **"What I say"** (his period voice) and **"Albert's Words"** — the project's dual-register device, the latter name nodding to biographer Albert Bigelow Paine. Twain is shown editing his own nineteenth-century phrasing for modern readers.
