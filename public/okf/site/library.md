# The Library (`/complete-works`)

The reading room and the visible face of the [corpus](/corpus/rag-corpus.md): "the material I lean on when we talk." Each entry opens to a full text. The page warns that Twain does not promise to hold the line the analysts have drawn over the decades — interpretation here is deliberately fluid.

## Categories

- **Novels & Major Works** — The Innocents Abroad (1869) through the Mysterious Stranger manuscripts.
- **The Complete Correspondence** — six letter volumes spanning 1853–1910.
- **Short Fiction** and **Other Stories & Sketches**.
- **Essays & Speeches** — including [What Is Man?](/works/what-is-man.md), Is Shakespeare Dead?, and the speeches.
- **Reference & Biography** — period encyclopedia entries about him.
- **News & Contemporary Accounts** — including the **1910 obituaries** (New York Times, San Francisco Call), reproduced as primary sources alongside the living-author conceit. See the [Reading Guide](/reading-guide.md) on holding those two facts together.
