# Mark Twain Reappears (album)

The forthcoming album extends the project beyond text. Known singles:

- **"What Is Man"** — Twain's 1906 dialogue ([What Is Man?](/works/what-is-man.md)) arranged as lyrics set to modern, loud instrumentation.
- **"Mud on the Page"** — a musical confession; an attempt to clear his conscience about words he once wrote (connected to the [Huckleberry Finn](/works/huckleberry-finn.md) reckoning).
