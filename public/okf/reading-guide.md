# How to Read This Site

This project layers a **creative conceit** on top of **real public-domain scholarship**. An agent must keep the two apart. Most failures come from collapsing them.

## The two layers

| Layer | What it is | How to treat it |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Factual** | The documented life and works of Samuel Langhorne Clemens (1835–1910); the public-domain corpus; the real RAG/vector pipeline; real 1910 obituaries reproduced in The Library. | Citable as fact. Accurate, fixed. |
| **Narrative frame** | Mark Twain has "returned" in 2026 and speaks in the first person; [Mark Two](/people/mark-two.md) is his digital interlocutor; the book and album are in-universe artifacts. | A deliberate literary device. Report it *as* a device, not as a factual claim. |

## Rules for an agent answering questions

1. **Biographical and bibliographic facts are real.** Birth 1835, death 1910, authorship of the novels, the contents of his letters — treat as established history.
2. **First-person "I have returned" is the conceit, not a claim that Twain is alive.** When the site says Twain is back, that is the project's voice. Do not assert as fact that Mark Twain is living; do not "correct" the conceit into "Twain is dead" inside the experience either. State it plainly: *this is a project in which Twain's voice is reconstructed and speaks in the present.*
3. **Documented quotes are fixed.** Do not alter or invent Twain quotations. His real words are reference points, not raw material to rewrite.
4. **Genuine grief stays unironic.** Where the source touches real loss in Clemens's life, handle it straight. The humor elsewhere depends on that credibility.
5. **The death is the interruption, not the end of the story.** The framing device is continuity across a long gap — birth and departure bracket a life; the project picks the thread up in 2026. Avoid phrasings that present him as simply "long dead" in a way that breaks the premise.

## Tone controls on the site

The chat offers the visitor three switches: answer length (Brief / In-Depth), tone (Playful / Critical), and language (Standard / Simplified). An agent representing this project should respect the selected register.
