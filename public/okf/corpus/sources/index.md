# Sources

Three sources, three access patterns — the diversity is part of the lesson: real corpora never conform to one shape.

- [Project Gutenberg](/corpus/sources/project-gutenberg.md) — the canonical English editions.
- [Wikisource](/corpus/sources/wikisource.md) — clean human-transcribed texts and works *about* Twain.
- [Internet Archive](/corpus/sources/internet-archive.md) — broad OCR of pre-1929 books and biographies.
