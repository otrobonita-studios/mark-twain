# Internet Archive

Broad, mid-quality OCR of pre-1929 books and monographs. Queried by subject and date range, capped at ~200 items to keep volume sane, with the `internetarchive` Python library handling downloads. This is where the early twentieth-century biographies live — Paine, Howells, contemporary scholarship.
