# Wikisource

Human-transcribed, very clean, smaller in volume. For works *about* Twain there is no category; instead the author page carries a hand-curated "Works about Twain" section, read via the MediaWiki API. Yields encyclopedia entries (1911 Britannica, 1879 American Cyclopaedia, Collier's, Appletons'), biographical sketches, and obituaries from the New York Times and the San Francisco Call.
