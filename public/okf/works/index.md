# Works

The Library on the site (`/complete-works`) organizes the corpus into categories: **Novels & Major Works**, **The Complete Correspondence** (six letter volumes, 1853–1910), **Short Fiction**, **Other Stories & Sketches**, **Essays & Speeches**, **Reference & Biography**, and **News & Contemporary Accounts** (including 1910 obituaries reproduced as primary sources).

This bundle seeds the most load-bearing works; the full catalogue lives in [The Library](/site/library.md) and the [corpus](/corpus/rag-corpus.md).
