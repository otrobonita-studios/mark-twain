# The Adventures of Tom Sawyer

Novel, 1876. Boyhood on the Mississippi, set in the town that became St. Petersburg in Twain's fiction.

## Why it recurs in this project

The famous fence-whitewashing episode is reused as a lens on the modern web. In [Debrief of a Dead](/project/debrief-of-a-dead.md), the internet is described as a gigantic fence the public queues up to whitewash for free — posting, creating, performing — while the platforms own the brush concession and bill for the privilege. Tom, at least, traded a dead rat for the labour.
