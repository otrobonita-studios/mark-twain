# Life on the Mississippi

Memoir, 1883. Twain's account of learning the river as a steamboat pilot before the war. The book that explains the name: "mark twain" is the leadsman's call for two fathoms.

## Why it matters here

The river is the project's master metaphor — two banks, one current, a pilot who can only see his own side. That image is what [Mark Two](/people/mark-two.md) turns against Twain in the [Huckleberry Finn](/works/huckleberry-finn.md) exchange.
