# What Is Man?

Philosophical dialogue, 1906, published anonymously at first. A bleak Socratic exchange in which Twain argues that humans are machines moved by outside influence and self-interest — his late, unsparing view of human nature.

## Why it matters here

Its argument underwrites the whole project's question — has human nature changed? — and it is adapted directly into music: Twain describes setting the text "to modern light and loud instruments" on the album [Mark Twain Reappears](/project/album-mark-twain-reappears.md).
