# A Connecticut Yankee in King Arthur's Court

Novel, 1889. A nineteenth-century engineer wakes in Arthurian Britain and sets about modernizing it, with results that turn dark. Twain's most direct fable about technology, progress, and the violence that can ride along with them.

## Why it matters here

It is the period precedent for the project's modern-technology satire — the smartphone-as-"pocket engine," the "update" as a philosophy of permanent dissatisfaction — explored in [Debrief of a Dead](/project/debrief-of-a-dead.md).
