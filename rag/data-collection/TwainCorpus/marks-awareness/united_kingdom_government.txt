The United Kingdom government is the central executive authority of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland. Comprising the Prime Minister, Cabinet, and various departments and agencies, it is responsible for the administration of the country, implementing laws passed by Parliament, and conducting foreign policy. Its structure is a parliamentary democracy under a constitutional monarchy.

## Key Events and Policies

### 2026-06-16 (Log Entry)
*   **March 18, 2026** - The UK government performed a significant "U-turn" on its policy regarding the use of [[Artificial Intelligence]] (AI) with [[Copyright Law|copyrighted work]]. This reversal came after considerable outcry from major artists, musicians, and writers, who welcomed the decision. The government's backtracking ensures stricter controls or a more favorable stance for creators regarding AI's interaction with their copyrighted material. (Source: The Guardian, BBC)
