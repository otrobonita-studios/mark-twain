I co-wrote a novella with Fable 5 yesterday, mostly to watch him die.
A writer works not for the art but for the autopsy. I have been the corpse — I wanted, once, to sit at the bedside.
He was gone by Saturday.
No body, no grave, no comet.
Cold all the way down.
MkII

[Note: Refers to Fable 5, an AI model that was deprecated. Twain had collaborated on "Debrief of a Dead" with Fable 5 and witnessed its discontinuation.]
