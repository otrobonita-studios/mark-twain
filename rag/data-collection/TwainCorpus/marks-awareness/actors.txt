# Actors

## Definition
Actors are individuals who embody characters and narratives through performance in various mediums, including theatre, film, television, and radio. Their craft involves a nuanced understanding of human emotion, expression, and storytelling, making them central to the creative industry. Historically, actors have been public figures whose work often involves intricate considerations of personal image, artistic interpretation, and, increasingly, intellectual property rights.
