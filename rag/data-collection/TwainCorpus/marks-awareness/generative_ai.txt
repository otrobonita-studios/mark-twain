## Generative AI

Generative AI refers to a category of artificial intelligence models capable of producing new content, such as text, images, audio, or code, often based on patterns learned from vast datasets. These models can "generate" novel outputs that mimic human creativity or style.

### Contradictions and Open Questions

The emergence of Generative AI has brought a significant conflict regarding `[[copyright_law]]` and the definition of 'fair use'. Tech companies champion the notion that training AI on public data falls under fair use, allowing for technological advancement without direct compensation to original creators. However, authors and publishers strongly contend that this practice constitutes `[[copyright_infringement]]` and demands a system of licensing and compensation for the intellectual property consumed by these models. Recent events, including lawsuits against major tech companies and the US Supreme Court's decision to decline a hearing on AI copyright, underscore the ongoing legal and ethical disputes. The question of how to balance innovation with creators' rights remains a pressing and unresolved matter, prompting governments worldwide to consider new policy frameworks.
