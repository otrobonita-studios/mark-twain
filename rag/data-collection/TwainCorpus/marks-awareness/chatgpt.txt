ChatGPT is a prominent example of [[Artificial Intelligence]] (AI) capable of generating human-like text, including stories, poems, and letters. It is developed by [[OpenAI]].

## Mark Twain's Views
Mark Twain views ChatGPT as a "modern steam-powered loom for sentences," a "clever apparatus that weaves words together, stealing the threads from every author." He asserts it lacks "soul or original wit" and considers it a "grand counterfeiter" that mimics humanity without genuine experience. He vehemently opposes its operation, particularly in the context of [[copyright]] infringement, describing it as the "grandest plagiarism ever conceived by human greed" and a "mechanical syndicate" that rents creators' brains back to them. He recalls his lifetime commitment to fighting for authors' [[copyright]] protections.
