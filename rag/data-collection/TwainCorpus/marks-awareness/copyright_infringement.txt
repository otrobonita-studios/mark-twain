Copyright infringement refers to the unauthorized use of works protected by copyright law, which grants creators exclusive rights over their original works for a limited time. This includes reproducing, distributing, performing, or displaying the copyrighted work without permission from the copyright holder.

### Contradictions and Open Questions

*   **Fair Use vs. Licensing for AI Training:** A central unresolved question is whether the use of copyrighted material to train generative [[Artificial Intelligence]] models falls under the doctrine of '[[Fair Use]]'. Tech companies argue it does, while authors and publishers contend that it requires explicit licensing and compensation, framing the lack thereof as copyright infringement. This legal and ethical debate is ongoing, with significant implications for both creators and the future of AI development, further emphasized by the US Supreme Court's recent decision to not hear a related case.
