FAQs
Are Booking.com Coupon Codes Available for Flights and Car Hire, or Just Hotels?
Booking.com sells flights, car rental, and airport taxis alongside accommodation, but most public discount codes are hotel-specific. Always check what a code applies to before assuming it covers your whole trip.
Do Booking.com Discounts Apply to "Getaway Deals" or Already-Discounted Listings?
Usually not. Properties already marked down through Booking.com's own deals often exclude additional codes, since you can't stack promotional discounts on top of another promotion.
What Is the Booking.com Genius Program?
Genius is Booking.com's free loyalty tier. Once you've stayed a certain number of times, you unlock automatic discounts (typically 10% or more) on eligible properties, plus perks like free breakfast or room upgrades — no code needed, it applies automatically at checkout.
Can I Combine a Discount Code With Genius Savings?
It depends on the property and offer terms. Genius discounts are usually baked into the displayed price, while promo codes are applied separately, so it's worth checking the final total before confirming.
