Wireless charging makes a daily task just a little bit easier, and Google’s versatile Pixelsnap Charger with Stand is one of the best wireless chargers at full price. And right now, it's at a 50 percent discount, making it a bargain worth bagging.

Mirroring Google’s curvy Pixel range with a rounded form and soft-touch finish, the Pixelsnap is a regular puck-style Qi2 25W magnetic charger that usually costs $40 on its own. Right now, you can get it and the curvy stand that it pops into for just $35. Perfect if you want to prop your phone up on the nightstand and have it double as an alarm clock, for example.

If you’re not familiar, Qi2 wireless charging is like Apple’s MagSafe; it enables you to stick your phone onto a charger using a ring of magnets, ensuring perfect alignment for the most efficient and speedy wireless charging possible. I love being able to stick my phone onto the charger at night without fumbling around for a cable.

The Pixelsnap sports the very latest Qi2 25W release, so it can charge supported phones, like the Pixel 10 Pro XL, Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra, and the iPhone 17 series at up to 25 watts. The Pixelsnap can also charge older iPhones, the rest of the Pixel 10 range, and various other Samsung phones at 15 watts.

Speed might not be the most important thing for you if you charge overnight, like I do, but the Pixelsnap Charger with Stand also has a smart design that’s pretty handy when your phone is running low at the end of the day: you can pop the charger puck out, and it will stick to the back of your phone so you can continue to scroll while it charges.

With the Pixel 11 range just around the corner, Google may be prepping a new charger, but it’s likely that the Pixelsnap will work with the new phones, too. The only thing missing is the power adapter, but if you need one, the Anker Nano 30W ($16) is very affordable.
