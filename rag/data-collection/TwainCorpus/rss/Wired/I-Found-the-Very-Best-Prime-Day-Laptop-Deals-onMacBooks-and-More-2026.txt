The Surface Laptop has been one of my favorite laptops since its relaunch in 2024. Lately, though, it's seen some untimely price hikes, which has made it harder to recommend. But here comes Prime Day to save the day with what is easily the best laptop deal of the week so far. The 2024 Surface Laptop is down 37 percent, dropping the price to $835. That puts its price below the starting configuration of the MacBook Air.
And what you get in exchange is a beautifully-made, portable and high-end Windows laptop that feels every bit as sturdy as a MacBook. The keyboard is top-notch, and the trackpad uses a haptic feedback engine, just like the MacBook Air. If you haven't used a Windows laptop in a while, you'll be blown away by how closely laptops like the Surface Laptop compare with the modern MacBook, even when it comes to performance and battery life. And in my experience, the Surface Laptop even has a couple of features that make it superior to the MacBook Air. The 3:2 aspect ratio of the display is glorious, which is my favorite feature. That taller screen size gives you more vertical space to work with, which I find both enjoyable while browsing the web and useful for work.
There's one important caveat, though. This configuration only comes with 256 GB of storage. Microsoft no longer technically sells this model, which is partially why prices have risen on the base configurations. There's also a 
new (much more expensive) model
 that just became available with the Snapdragon X2 chip inside. But when it comes to 
fantastic tech deals

Laptops under $750 have a bad reputation, and for the most part, they've earned it themselves. But there are some standouts, the most notable being the HP OmniBook 3. It's on sale for under $550, despite being a laptop with 16 GB of RAM and 512 GB of storage. Those are incredible specs for laptop at this price. To put things in perspective, that's double the memory and storage you get in the more expensive MacBook Neo.
Of course, there are a number of compromises, where the HP OmniBook 3 doesn't measure up to something like the MacBook Neo. The screen is nowhere near as sharp, especially since the this is a larger, 16-inch display. Then, there's the touchpad. It feels cheap and clumsy to use. But when it comes to battery life and performance, the OmniBook 3 is really impressive for a laptop of this price.
