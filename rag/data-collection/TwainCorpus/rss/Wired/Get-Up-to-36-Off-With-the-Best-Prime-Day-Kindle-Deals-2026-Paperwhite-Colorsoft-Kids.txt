There's a pretty
 easy rule to buying a Kindle: Wait for a sale. Amazon's Prime Day Kindle deals, which happen every summer and fall, plus other sale events like Black Friday, always offer the best prices. While Amazon's summer 
Prime Days
 were in July in previous years, this year they arrived early, in June, and will run for almost a full week from June 23 until June 26.
Any day during that sale is a great one to claim your own new 
Kindle e-reader
 from Amazon. The on-sale Kindles usually don't sell out, but if there's a specific color or storage size you want, you might want to snap that up early in case that specific version does sell out for a little bit. The 
newest Kindle Scribes
 (including the Kindle Scribe Colorsoft!) also went on sale, which I'm thrilled to see since they have such a hefty price tag compared to older models. Here are the best Kindles that are on sale right now, plus some great on-sale Kindle accessories if you already have an Amazon e-reader of your own.

Absolute Best Prime Day Deals

Amazon Prime Day live blog
 for real-time updates.
