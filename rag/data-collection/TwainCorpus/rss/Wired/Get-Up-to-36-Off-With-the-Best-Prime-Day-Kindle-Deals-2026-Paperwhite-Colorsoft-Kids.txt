There's a pretty
 easy rule to buying a Kindle: Wait for a sale. Amazon's Prime Day Kindle deals, which happen every summer and fall, plus other sale events like Black Friday, always offer the best prices. While Amazon's summer 
Prime Days
 were in July in previous years, this year they arrived early, in June, and will run for almost a full week from June 23 until June 26.
Any day during that sale is a great one to claim your own new 
Kindle e-reader
 from Amazon. The on-sale Kindles usually don't sell out, but if there's a specific color or storage size you want, you might want to snap that up early in case that specific version does sell out for a little bit. So far, there are no 
Kindle Scribes
 planned to go on sale, but it would be nice to see options like the expensive 
Kindle Scribe Colorsoft
 available for cheaper. Here are the best Kindles that are on sale right now.
Also be sure to browse our roundup of the 
Absolute Best Prime Day Deals
, or dip into our 
Amazon Prime Day live blog
 for real-time updates.
