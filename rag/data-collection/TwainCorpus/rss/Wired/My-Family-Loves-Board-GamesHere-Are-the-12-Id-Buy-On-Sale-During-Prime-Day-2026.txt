One of the 
best family board games
 for young kids, Labyrinth is easy to grasp. The game board is a maze, and you must collect treasures dictated by the cards you are dealt. Designed by a German psychologist, the fun comes from changing the maze by pushing a tile onto the board, which displaces another each turn. You can carve a route for yourself, but also think about how to block your opponents; the best moves do both.

A great game for larger groups, Herd Mentality is all about writing down the same answer as everyone else.
