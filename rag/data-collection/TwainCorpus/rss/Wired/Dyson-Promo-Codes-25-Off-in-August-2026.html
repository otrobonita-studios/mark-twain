Unlock the Best Dyson Airwrap Promos

Like all of Dyson’s tech lineup, Dyson hair tools have the signature high-power, efficiency, sleek design, … and high price tag. One of Dyson’s most iconic and history-withstanding tools is the Dyson Airwrap, which debuted back in 2018. We rated it a 9/10 WIRED Recommends, for its multifunctionality, different offerings for different hair types, less heat use than previous models, and light and easy-to-use design.

Dyson's Airwrap Is a Salon-Worthy Hair Transmogrifier
It can curl, it can smooth, it can dry. But it comes with Dyson's signature price tag.
By Medea Giordano

When it comes to hair gadgets, there are many gimmicks that claim to make styling easy and effortless, but the Dyson Airwrap actually works, using air to suck strands of your hair around the curling barrels to produce a lasting result without subjecting your hair to too much damaging heat. It's a multifunction styler, but the Airwrap really shines as a curling iron. Using its internal motor, it creates a vortex of air inside the hollow barrel powerful enough to draw your hair to it. All you have to do is place the end of your hair near the barrel, and the machine does the gripping and wrapping for you, so you don't have to worry about touching a hot curling barrel.
