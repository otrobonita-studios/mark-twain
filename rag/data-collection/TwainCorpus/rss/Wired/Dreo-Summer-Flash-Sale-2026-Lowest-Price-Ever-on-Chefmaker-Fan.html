A Dreo Flash sale from August 1 to 3 is a deal that made me do an actual double-take: half off the Dreo Chefmaker combi air fryer. The Chefmaker is one of the most interesting pieces of cooking tech I reviewed last year: the only air fryer I’d ever cook a steak in. Or a roast. Or a pork chop.

That’s because it doesn’t work like other air fryers. It’s got a built-in probe and steam cooking—so it can bring any meat up slowly to the desired temperature, then reverse “sear” (probably just brown) your meat with a heat blast in the final step. It works like a charm, honestly.

And yet, it’s still also a basket-style fryer that’ll crisp wings, Tater tots, or french fries. It’s also a combi cooker, which means it aerates the oven with steam to lock in moisture on large cuts of meat. And it’s also an app-controlled probe cooker, which you can set to slowly bring meat up to temp before finishing with a blast of heat.

Anyway, it’s half-price right now for three days only, from August 1 to 3 on Dreo’s website. This is the lowest price I’ve ever seen for this device, and the lowest price I expect to see.

But the 12-speed TurboCool 765S misting tower fan borrows tech from Dreo’s humidifiers in order to diffuse ultrafine particles of mist. Small, like 17 microns small.
