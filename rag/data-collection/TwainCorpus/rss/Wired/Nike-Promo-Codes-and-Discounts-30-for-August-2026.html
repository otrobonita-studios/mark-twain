Nike has been having a bit of a comeback—“comeback” might be generous, but the sentiment is there. In 2024, the sportswear giant faced restructuring struggles, accusations of sexism, and layoffs exceeding two percent of its staff. After difficult years marked by falling sales, poor innovation, and strained retail partnerships, CEO John Donahoe resigned, and company veteran Elliott Hill returned in his place. Under Hill’s leadership, Nike is shifting focus back to its core market, working to repair relationships with retailers, athletes, and creatives.

Despite these shortcomings, Nike remains one of the most popular brands worldwide. Having worked at a Nike outlet for a year, I can tell you that unless you need something immediately, paying full price is rarely worthwhile. Most Nike products eventually go on sale through seasonal discounts or promotions. And in the meantime, I’ve rounded up some of the current best Nike coupons and Nike promo codes to help you save.
