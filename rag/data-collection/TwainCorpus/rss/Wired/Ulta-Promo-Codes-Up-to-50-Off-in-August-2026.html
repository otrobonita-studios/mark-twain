Use Ulta Beauty Rewards for Ulta Discounts

The Ulta Beauty Rewards program is free to join and offers many perks with minimal effort required from you. First, you earn points for every dollar you spend. The program has multiple tiers: as a standard member, you earn 1 point per dollar; platinum members earn 1.25 points, and diamond members earn 1.5 points. These points can be redeemed for discounts, and the best part is that they do not expire. During your birthday month, you get even more special perks. These include earning extra points on every qualifying purchase, a free gift, and a $10 coupon exclusively for platinum and diamond members. Some other benefits include exclusive beauty deals, early access to sales, and full-size gifts for diamond members. Diamond members also get a $25 discount on beauty services and free shipping on orders over $25.

Why Is My Ulta Beauty Coupon Not Working?

One of the best ways to determine if your Ulta coupon isn’t working is to check the fine print—oftentimes, deals end on specific dates, so make sure you’re within that time frame. Also, make sure that the product you want to buy is included in the sale, as many items and brands aren’t eligible on certain offers. If you still aren’t sure why, reach out to support for further help.

Does Ulta Offer Student, Military, or Healthcare Worker Discounts?

Ulta doesn't currently have a standing student, military, or healthcare discount program. Savings mainly come through Ultamate Rewards and sale events instead.

How Do Ulta Reward Points Work?

Members earn 1 point per $1 spent (2 points/$1 for Diamond and Platinum tiers). Points convert to redeemable dollars off, usually in $3–$50 increments, and typically expire at year-end if unused.

Are Ulta Coupons Stackable?

Ulta allows one coupon per transaction, though reward point redemptions can often be combined with a separate promo code. Coupons usually can't be combined with each other.
