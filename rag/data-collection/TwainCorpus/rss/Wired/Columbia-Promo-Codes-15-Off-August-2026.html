When I decided to take a last-minute ski trip, despite never having skied before, I went straight to Columbia. Was I shredding the slopes? Absolutely not, but at least I was warm, thanks to my Snowy Summit Insulated Jacket and Bugaboo II Pants. Here at WIRED, we’re fans of Columbia. The brand makes reliable, well-priced outdoor gear for skiing, hiking, hunting, or just a day at the beach. Plus, there’s almost always a deal to be found.

WIRED reviewer Chris Haslam loves the Columbia Konos Trillium ATR as a value all-around trail-running shoe for faster hikes, day trips, and mixed terrain excursions. The plush cushioning and 8-millimeter drop are ideal on packed dirt, gravel, and woodland paths. For hiking boots, he recommends the Tellurix Titanium OutDry, which are waterproof, offer serious traction on wet rock and mud, and are surprisingly lightweight at 1.6 pounds per pair.

With summer approaching, now’s the time to prepare for outings, and if you’re eyeing snow gear, the off-season is when prices hit their lowest. I’ve gathered the latest Columbia promo codes to sweeten the deal.
