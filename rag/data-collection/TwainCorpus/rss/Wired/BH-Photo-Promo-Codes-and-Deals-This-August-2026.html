FAQs

Does B&H Price Match?

Yes, you’re in luck! B&H has a price match guarantee against major authorized retailers (like Adorama or Best Buy) for identical, in-stock items.

Are B&H's "Used" and "Open-Box" Deals Worth it?

B&H grades use gear ratings like "8/10" or "9/10" condition, and often include a warranty period, making it an easy way to get name-brand equipment below new pricing.

What Is B&H's Return Policy?

Most items can be returned within 30 days in original condition, just make sure you check the fine print.

When Are B&H's Biggest Sale Events?

Make sure you check back often during holidays, like Black Friday/Cyber Monday, Presidents' Day, and manufacturer-driven promotions (Sony, Canon, Nikon rebate periods), which typically offer the biggest discounts.
