More Deals I've Spotted

I'll be looking for more great vacuum deals all week long. Here are a few more you might want to check out.

Bissell PowerClean Cordless Stick Vacuum for $150 ($50 off): This is the cheaper version of the Bissell FurFinder vacuum. It has a weaker headlight and no FurFinder attachment, but it's not priced low enough to be worth it. I'd just get the FurFinder above, since you'll get more features for the same price.

Dyson Ball Animal 3 Extra for $400 ($150 off): If, for some reason, you really, really want an upright vacuum instead of a cordless model, then Dyson's Ball Animal 3 Extra is on sale. The “Extra” in the name means it comes with twice the attachments compared to the regular Ball Animal 3. It's designed with pet hair in mind, and former WIRED reviewer Jaina Grey was happy with how well it vacuumed her rabbit's hair around the house.

Ecovacs Deebot X12 OmniCyclone for $800 ($600 off): Ecovacs is the parent company of both Deebot and Yeedi, but Yeedi is usually cheaper with some similar features. Still, we've liked past Deebot robot vacuums we've tried, and this is a good deal on one of its premium models. I haven't tested it yet, but I like that you can mark spots in the app as extra dirty so the robot vacuum knows to use its pre-spray on stains and messes before it starts mopping.

Ecovacs Deebot T90 Pro Omni for $500 ($400 off): If you're looking for more options in the $500 range, the Deebot T90 Pro Omni is also on sale for Prime Day to bring it down into that range. It's usually on sale for $700, so this is a better deal than usual. It's another one I have yet to test, but promises a self-cleaning roller mop, edge cleaning, and impressive 30,000 Pa of suction.

Roborock Saros 20 for $1,300 ($300 off): This is Roborock's newest and most powerful robot vacuum yet, with 36,000 Pa of suction. Former WIRED reviewer Adrienne So didn't love that the vacuum could leave debris behind while it moved from her hard floors to her carpet, though, and the Adaptlift technology couldn't lift itself out of her fireplace area. Still, if you have fewer transition spaces you're worried about, she was happy with this robot vacuum otherwise.
