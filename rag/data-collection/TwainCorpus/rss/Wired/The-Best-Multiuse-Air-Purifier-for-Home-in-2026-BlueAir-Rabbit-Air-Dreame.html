If you're tight on space or just want a more aesthetically pleasing machine, the best air purifier for home depends on your needs.

If you live in a small apartment like me, you may need to have a multifunctional air purifier for a practical reason: space. These compact, dual-purpose machines are useful if you are tight on square footage or don't want to shell out the money for two separate appliances. Or maybe you're aesthetically driven and want something that looks cool in your space rather than a clunky, large air purifier that can be an eyesore.

Regardless of the reason, these multiuse air purifiers look good, save space, and combine appliances into a single, energy-efficient device.

Why Choose a Multiuse Air Purifier?

Small-apartment dwellers need a multipurpose air purifier for a practical reason: space. These compact appliances handle two or more tasks to maximize efficiency, while possibly saving you money too. They also reduce cords, the need for outlets, and storage space when the machine is not in use during off-seasons. Or maybe you're more aesthetically driven and want something that looks cool in the space rather than a clunky, large air purifier.

How I Tested

I tested each model for nearly a month, moving it around my house to test in different locations: when cooking on my unventilated gas stove, under my living room window that draws in a lot of building and exhaust fumes, and outside my cat's litter box. I measured whether and when the air purifier auto-engages, and how long it takes to return to clean air quality. I also used an air quality monitor to check for pollutants and the purifiers' efficacy, and a decibel monitor app on my phone to measure noise level when the appliance is on high. I also tested each setting, paying special attention to the multipurpose features and whether they were actually useful or just gimmicky. When another tester reviews a machine, they use the same metrics.

What I Look For in a Dual-Purpose Air Purifier

One of the main things I considered when testing was whether the extra features were actually useful. Each recommendation on the list has been deemed successful in both air purification and its other marketed “dual-purpose” function. I also considered size and purification ability—that's why the fun terrarium isn't a top pick; while it's super cute, it's not as effective as an air purifier.
