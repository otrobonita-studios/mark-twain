The best Android charger might seem obvious, but it can depend heavily on your priorities. Do you want to charge your phone from zero to 100 as quickly as possible? Are you charging at home in bed overnight, or on the go in a hotel? WIRED Reviews staffers are constantly testing Android chargers, and below you'll find our top recommendations for every situation.

There was once a time when different phones required different cords, like Lightning cables for iPhone or Micro-USB for Android. But now, the majority of devices don't have special ports and just use a USB-C cable. Modern smartphones from Apple, Google, and Samsung use the Power Delivery charging standard, a fast-charging protocol for USB-C that supports higher voltage and wattage to charge phones faster. All the chargers below support USB-C PD and will work with any smartphone ecosystem, including iPhone.

How Fast Can My Android Phone Charge?

Charging speeds depend on the equipment you use. If you aren't using cables or wireless chargers that can handle these speeds, your experience may vary. The same goes for your phone usage—if you're using your phone while it's charging, or while it's hot, or both, your speeds might diminish. But generally, the maximum charging speeds are as follows:

  • Google Pixel 10: 45 watts (wired, Pro XL), 30 watts (wired, 10, 10a, and 10 Pro)
  • Samsung Galaxy S26: 60 watts (wired, Galaxy S26 Ultra), 45 watts (wired, most Plus and Ultra models), 25 watts (wired, most base models)

Top charging speeds depend heavily on which phone you're using. Read more about fast charging here.

What Wattage Do I Need?

Truly, at the end of the day, it doesn't matter that much. A 15-watt charger is the bare minimum we'd recommend, and it will work just fine to charge your phone in a couple of hours, which is better for long-term battery health and perfect for charging overnight or while you're chilling on the couch. If you need as much juice as fast as possible, a 40-watt charger will do the trick and can get most newer smartphones to 80 percent charged in about half an hour. Most of us prefer to have a couple of different options around to use situationally. Note that you shouldn't use a charger under 15 watts unless your device specifically calls for it. It'll just be slow and frustrating otherwise.
