Google may spend years testing the durability of its own hardware, but because accidents happen, you’d still be wise to get a good case for your Pixel 10 phone. Most devices in the Pixel 10 line—the Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, and Pixel 10 Pro Fold—use Corning's Gorilla Glass Victus 2. (The Pixel 10a uses Gorilla Glass 7i.) It's tough glass, but it's not indestructible, nor is it as strong as what you'd find on other flagship phones, like Samsung's Galaxy S26 Ultra or Apple's iPhone 17 Pro, which embeds ceramic nanocrystals into the glass for extra strength.

No matter which Google Pixel 10 you have, you'll want to protect your device with a case at the very minimum (a screen protector is also a smart buy). This will decrease the odds of an accidental drop ruining your smartphone and preserve the phone’s appearance so it looks great over time. It’s an especially important detail if you plan on trading in the phone for a newer device in the future.

I've tested several Pixel 10 cases to find the best. I’ve included other accessories in this guide, as well, including some magnetic Qi2 gadgets. To get the most out of your Pixel 10, be sure to check out our guide to Key Pixel Settings to Change.

Will Pixel 9 Cases Fit on the Pixel 10 Series?

Sometimes the stars align, and an updated phone model has the identical dimensions, shape, and design as the previous model, allowing you to use an older case. That is, unfortunately, not the case with the Pixel 10 series. Google updated the speakers on this line, so while you may be able to fit some soft Pixel 9 cases over the Pixel 10 phones, you'll run into other issues, namely blocking the speaker port. Pixel 9 cases without magnets will interrupt the Qi2 functionality on the Pixel 10 series. For the best experience, I recommend avoiding a Pixel 9 case altogether, whether it's for the Pixel 10 or Pixel 10 Pro.

Will Pixel 10 Cases Fit on the Pixel 10 Pro?

Most Pixel 10 cases will fit the Pixel 10 Pro, and vice versa. Several retailers market their cases (even Google) for both phones. I’ve tested several Pixel 10 Pro cases on the Pixel 10 and have had no issues. The cutouts are all accurate, the button placements are perfect, and the fit is snug. However, Pixel 10 and 10 Pro cases are not compatible with the Pixel 10 Pro XL.

Will Pixel 10 Cases Fit on the Pixel 10a?

Nope. The Pixel 10a is slightly taller, thicker, and wider than the Pixel 10, and the camera bumps are different, too. These cases are not interchangeable.

What Does ‘Qi2’ Mean for Pixel 10 Cases?

The Pixel 10 phones are the first Android flagships with native Qi2 wireless charging. That means magnets are built into the backs of the phones, just like Apple's iPhones and MagSafe, allowing you to access faster, more efficient wireless charging. Magnets also align the coils perfectly, eliminating any worries about your phone not charging because you didn't place it just right on a charging pad.

Not every Pixel 10 case has magnets baked in, though most of them do. (Almost all of the cases in this guide feature magnets.) However, I have asked nearly every case maker whether its cases are Qi2-certified by the Wireless Power Consortium (which manages the Qi2 standard), and the answer is almost universally no, which may point to less reliable and efficient charging. For the best experience, make sure to check the product listing to see if it mentions “Qi2,” “Made for Google,” or “Pixelsnap compatible,” which is Google's name for its magnetic accessory system. The latter two certifications at least ensure that the case was subject to a rigorous testing standard.

One neat feature to note: When you place your Pixel 10 on a Qi2 wireless charger, you should see an option to add a screensaver. This isn't a traditional screensaver, though you can totally just set a clock or photo and call it a day. There's also a Home Controls option, which lets you place your Google Home smart devices—like smart lights and smart shades—on the lock screen while the phone is charging for quick access. Head to Settings > Display & touch > Screen saver to set it up.

Will My Old Wireless Charger Work for the Pixel 10?

While Qi2 is backward compatible with Qi wireless charging, I have extensive evidence that not all Qi wireless chargers are fully compatible with Qi2 phones. The reason may come down to the placement of the wireless charging array on newer devices. By all means, try charging your new Pixel 10 with your preferred case on a wireless charger you already own. If it works, great! It won't be as fast or efficient, but at least it'll work. However, if it doesn't work, look for a Qi2 charger. You can check out several picks here (and below).

How I Test Cases

I install every case I receive over the Pixel 10 series and take a look at the fit, whether or not ports or speakers are blocked, and ensure the buttons are clicky and responsive. I also monitor how much the case sticks out from the display—the taller the better, as it'll keep your display off the ground. To test Qi2 accessories, I gauge the strength of the magnetic connection. I also install every screen protector to test the ease of the application process.

I don't do drop tests because I have a limited number of Pixel phones. In my experience, though, a case isn’t a guarantee your phone won’t crack when dropped, regardless of “drop test” claims from a manufacturer. Even so, a case improves your chances of protection, especially if you add a screen protector.

Other Screen Protectors to Consider

Zagg Glass Elite, Glass Elite Privacy, and XTR4 for $50–$60: Zagg has a streamlined and intuitive installation process; my application was perfect.
