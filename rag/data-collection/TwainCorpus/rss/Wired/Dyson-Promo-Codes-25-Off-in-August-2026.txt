When it comes to hair gadgets, there are many gimmicks that claim to make styling easy and effortless, but the Dyson Airwrap actually works, using air to suck strands of your hair around the curling barrels to produce a lasting result without subjecting your hair to too much damaging heat. It's a multifunction styler, but the Airwrap really shines as a curling iron. Using its internal motor, it creates a vortex of air inside the hollow barrel powerful enough to draw your hair to it.
