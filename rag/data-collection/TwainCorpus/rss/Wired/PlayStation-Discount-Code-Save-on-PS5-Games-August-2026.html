Refurbished PlayStation Deals: Save Up to $100

One of the only ways to actually get a deal on a PlayStation is by buying a certified model. You don’t want to just go and buy through any retailer though, as some have different certifications and define the term “refurbished” differently. Fortunately, Sony has its certified refurbished program to get you PS5 discounts that you won’t regret buying later. These undergo a rigorous recertification process to ensure that they function as well as a brand new PlayStation. In other words, they should work like they’re new.

This includes up to $100 off consoles, such as the PlayStation 5 Digital Edition or the PlayStation 5 Slim. Sony also has refurbished DualShock controllers for sale.
