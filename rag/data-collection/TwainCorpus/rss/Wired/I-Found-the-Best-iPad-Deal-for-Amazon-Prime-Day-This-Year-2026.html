The iPad Air isn't the only iPad on sale for Prime Day, though. The next best discount is on the 2025 iPad mini. It's also down $100, bringing the price to $500. That's the cheapest it's been in at least the past few months. Unlike the iPad Air, this is purely a content consumption device. It excels at those times when you want to lean back on the couch or by a pool. As a tablet that's small enough to use one-handed, it's a great reading device, too.

The other two iPads in the lineup don't have as big a discount. Both sizes of the iPad Pro are $100 off, but that's what they normally sell for. The same is true of the base 11-inch iPad, which is a standard $50, selling for $299. There's nothing wrong with these iPads per se—in fact, the base iPad remains the best overall iPad that I think most people should buy. It's simple, but that's where the iPad shines as a tablet.

The iPad Air and iPad Pro, on the other hand, both really need accessories to make them feel like a complete product. Speaking of which, there are a number of my favorite iPad accessories on sale for Prime Day that are worth checking out, too.
