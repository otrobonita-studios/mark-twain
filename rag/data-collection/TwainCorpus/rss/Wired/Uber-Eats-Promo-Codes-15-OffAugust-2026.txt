FAQs
Does Uber Eats Offer Promo Codes for New Users?
Yes, Uber Eats regularly offers first-order discounts for new customers, often a percentage off or a capped dollar amount (like $15–$25 off) on your first delivery.
How Do I Apply a Promo Code on Uber Eats?
In the app, go to your account menu, select "Promotions" (or "Add promo/gift card" at checkout), enter the code, and it should apply automatically if valid and still active.
Does Uber Eats Offer Discounts on Grocery or Convenience Store Orders?
Yes, Uber Eats has expanded beyond restaurants to include grocery, convenience, and retail delivery, and many promo codes apply across these categories
Can I Use Uber Eats Codes With Uber (Rides) Credits?
No. Uber Eats and Uber rides promo codes/credits are generally separate, though Uber One membership benefits apply across both rides and Eats.
