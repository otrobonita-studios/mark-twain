FAQs

How Often Does Adidas Release New Discount Codes?

Codes tend to cluster around sales periods (like end-of-season sales, Black Friday, or new collection launches) rather than being released on a fixed schedule.

Can I Use a Discount Code on Sale or Outlet Items?

Often not. Codes are frequently restricted to full-price items, since outlet and clearance stock is already discounted. Check the code's terms and conditions, as "exclusions apply" is common wording for this.

What Is adiClub and Does It Give Discounts?

adiClub is Adidas's free loyalty programme. Members can get early access to product drops, birthday rewards, and occasional members-only discount codes.

How Do I Use an Adidas Discount Code?

Add your items to the bag, go to checkout, and look for the "Discount code" or "Promo code" box. Enter the code, apply it, and the reduction should show in your order summary before you pay.
