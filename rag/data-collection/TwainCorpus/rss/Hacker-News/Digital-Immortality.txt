 Winging their way over the internet to massive data centres, joining trillions of their brethren in the mad dash to get eaten

pieces, then stretched out over thousands of dimensions. And then fed into layer upon layer of transformation - poked in this way and that. Changing a weight here. A weight there.

Everything I write. Everything 
everybody
