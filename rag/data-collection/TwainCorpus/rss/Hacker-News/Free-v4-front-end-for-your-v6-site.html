Free v4 frontend for your v6 site

Getting started with three simple steps:

  1. Read the terms of service.
  2. Create an A record with the value 116.202.1.213 - next to your existing AAAA record.
  3. Spread the word.

Yes, that is really everything you need to do!

Why is Netiter providing this service for free?

We believe that when IANA ran out of IPv4 addresses in 2011, the internet should already have been converted to dual stack, such that new clients and servers could be deployed as IPv6 only without any problems. That has not happened.

We believe that owners of IPv6 only sites should not have to pay extra due to other parties' failure to upgrade in time. For that reason the service is free for website owners. However we will ask those internet providers who are sending IPv4 clients to us, to pay the cost of their traffic. If you are an internet provider, you can register for a free trial.
