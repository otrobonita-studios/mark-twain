Gloomberb
Open-source finance terminal. Fast, keyboard-driven and extensible. Available as a desktop app or TUI.
curl -fsSL gloomberb.com/install | bash
Open the pane you need. Keep moving.
Gloomberb is command-bar first. Type a ticker or a shortcut like
 
DES AAPL
or 
TOP
 and jump straight into the market view.
Research companies
Quotes, charts, financials, filings, holders, insiders, options, analyst ratings, events, and relative valuation.
Follow markets
Ranked stories, breaking news, sector feeds, global indices, FX, macro events, yield curves, movers, and sentiment.
Run a workspace
Portfolios, watchlists, broker connections, alerts, notes, AI screens, prediction markets, and Gloom Cloud chat.
