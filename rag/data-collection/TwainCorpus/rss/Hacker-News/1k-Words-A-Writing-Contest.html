How much is a picture really worth?

They say a picture is worth a thousand words.I say… Prove it.

[1] Look at this picture.
[2] Write 1,000 words about it by August 31st, 2026. You can write whatever you want, so long as you connect it to the photo. Creative nonfiction. Sci-fi. A joke. Something else. It’s up to you.
[3] The best writing wins $1,000 (of course).

This is a project by Quarter Mile (Jordan and Carter). You can email us here if you have any doubts or questions about the contest. We’ve run other contests in the past, like DEAR ALIENS (writing) and The Next Tiramisu (dessert).
