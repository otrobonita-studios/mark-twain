Privacy, Clearly

YOUR DATA STAYS. AI VISITS.

Your journal, memories, and profile live on your device — that is where the permanent record exists. When AI helps structure an entry or answer a question, processing is ephemeral. The product is designed so your archive remains yours, portable, and not platform data.

          Read Privacy Policy

01

Stored on your device. Entries, semantic memory, remembered chats, and your evolving profile never leave your phone as stored data.

02

AI content is processed using zero-data-retention controls and is not used to train AI models. Limited technical request metadata may still be processed or retained.

03

Remembering is explicit. You choose whether a chat becomes part of memory or stays off the record.

04

Export or wipe anytime. Keep a local JSON/CSV backup, move your memories elsewhere, or bring them to another AI tool if you choose. No account required — no server archive to delete.
