Petals

        Run large language models at home, BitTorrent‑style

          Generate text with 
Llama 3.1
 (up to 405B), 
Mixtral
 (8x22B), 
Falcon
 (40B+) or 
BLOOM
 (176B)
          and fine‑tune them for your tasks — using a consumer-grade GPU or Google Colab.

          You load a part of the model, then join a
          
network

          of people serving its other parts.
          Single‑batch inference runs at up to 
6 tokens/sec
 for 
Llama 2
 (70B) and
          up to 
4 tokens/sec
 for 
Falcon
 (180B) — enough for
          
chatbots
 and interactive apps.

          Beyond classic LLM APIs —
          you can employ any fine-tuning and sampling methods, execute custom paths through the model, or see its hidden states.
          You get the comforts of an API with the flexibility of 
PyTorch
 and 🤗 
Transformers
.
