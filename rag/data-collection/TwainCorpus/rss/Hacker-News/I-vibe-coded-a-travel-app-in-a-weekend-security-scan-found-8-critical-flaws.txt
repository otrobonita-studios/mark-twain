TripNest

A role-based group travel planner — plan trips, assign permissions, and keep everything (itinerary, flights, packing lists, chat) in one place instead of scattered across Notes apps and group chats.

🔗 
Live app:
 
https://trip-together-planner-21.lovable.app/

Built in a weekend with 
Lovable
, then security-tested with 
Perfai Security
 before release.

Why

Group trip planning usually falls apart the same way every time:

Itineraries end up in one person's Notes app

Flight info gets shared as screenshots buried in a group chat

There's no safe way to let people 
view
 the plan without risking someone accidentally editing or deleting something

Trip discussion gets lost in unrelated chat noise

TripNest solves this with a proper role system and one dedicated space per trip.

Roles

Role

Permissions

Admin

Full control — add/remove participants and planners, edit everything

Planner

Can build out the itinerary, can't remove other travelers

Participant

View-only — follows the plan, can't change dates or delete flights

Features

Trip creation (name, location, start/end date)

Role-based invites with accept-before-join

Collaborative day-by-day itinerary planning

Location tracking

Flight info storage

Packing lists (item + quantity)

Per-trip group messaging thread

Responsive UI

Security

The initial deployment was scanned with Perfai Security (tests the live deployed app — routes, roles, and requests — rather than just reviewing source code). The first scan surfaced 8 critical authorization issues that weren't visible from normal UI use. All were fixed and confirmed resolved on a follow-up scan.

If you're building on top of this or forking it, 
do not treat "it works in the UI" as proof the permission model is enforced correctly at the data layer
 — verify role restrictions hold across every table/endpoint, not just the components that reference them.
