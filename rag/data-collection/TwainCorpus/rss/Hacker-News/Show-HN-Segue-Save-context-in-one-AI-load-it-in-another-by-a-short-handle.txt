FAQ

Fair 
questions

What exactly is a handle?

Three pronounceable syllables — like 
brelavo
 — minted when a context is saved. It's the context's address: case-insensitive, unique, and resolvable only inside your account.

Which AIs work with Segue?

Any client that speaks MCP with streamable HTTP and OAuth — Claude (Desktop, Code), ChatGPT, Cursor, Windsurf, Codex, and more as the ecosystem grows. Setup is pasting one prompt: the AI configures itself and walks you through sign-in.

What is MCP?

The Model Context Protocol — an open standard, stewarded by the Linux Foundation, that lets AI assistants connect to outside tools. Segue is one server that all your assistants share.

Could someone else load my handle?

No. Handles only resolve for clients signed in to your account. Outside it, a handle is just a made-up word.

Does Segue read or auto-capture my conversations?

Never. Nothing is saved unless an AI you connected calls 
save_context
 in front of you, and nothing loads unless you pass the handle. No ambient memory, no surprises.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your account drops to the free tier. Everything already saved stays loadable, editable, exportable, and deletable — only 
new
 saves pause while you're over the free cap.
