Frequently Asked Questions

Is Silent Shark available now?

Yes. Silent Shark 1.0 is available now on Steam.

Which platforms are supported?

The Steam release supports Windows, macOS, and Linux. iOS, iPadOS, and Android versions are planned separately.

Can I play it now?

Yes. The free demo is playable instantly in your browser and is also available to download from Steam.

What is included in the full game?

              The full game adds the Pacific campaign, patrol and career progression, six submarine classes, the complete scenario
              generator, historical events and upgrades, missions, and Steam achievements.

Is Silent Shark a 3D submarine simulator?

              Silent Shark is centered on a tactical chart and period-inspired instruments. Periscope, binocular, hydrophone, and TDC
              stations support observation and attack without turning the game into a free-roaming 3D simulation.

Which languages are available?

              The game supports English, French, Italian, German, Spanish, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, Simplified Chinese,
              Ukrainian, and Japanese interfaces.
