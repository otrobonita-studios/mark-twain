Introducing the Godox Transparent Viewfinder Camera C100—our first camera featuring a built-in light meter, wrapped in a bold transparent design that brings effortless, low-barrier shooting fun to everyday capture. Merging vintage toy-camera charm with smart digital utility, the C100 strips away the complexity of traditional exposure. It is crafted for film enthusiasts and lifestyle creators who crave an authentic, screen-free connection to the world without the pressure.

Unfiltered Vision, Pure Optical Viewfinder

Ditch the digital barrier. The C100 features a transparent optical viewfinder that lets you compose shots while staying fully engaged with your subject, bringing back the raw joy of real-world observation.

Master the Light, Center-Weighted Light Metering

The C100 reads brightness from the central 25% of the frame for instant, optimal exposure. More than a standalone camera, it doubles as a sleek, dependable external light meter—the perfect companion for film photography purists.

Seamless HUD, Smart Transparent Display

The optical transparent screen elegantly overlays essential shooting data—including current mode, framing grids, battery, and exposure parameters—directly onto your field of view without cluttering the aesthetic.

Vibe Over Pixels, Effortless Photo & Video

Forget pixel-perfection; focus on the fun. Built for spontaneous capturing, the Godox C100 strips away the pressure of professional gear. Whether snapping a quick photo or rolling a short video, it turns every unpolished, raw everyday moment into a playful memory.

Unbox Your Day, The Joy of "Blind" Shooting

Live in the moment and embrace the suspense. By removing instant screen playback, the Godox C100 invites you to shoot freely all day and experience the nostalgic excitement of "unboxing" your memories.

Cyber-Retro Aesthetic, Elevate Your Carry

With its transparent display and retro design, the C100 is as much a fashion accessory as it is a camera. It is the ultimate statement piece for anyone looking to showcase their unique style.

Frame Your Mood, Four Creative Aspect Ratios

Shape your visual narrative on the fly. Switch seamlessly between 16:9 cinematic bars, classic 4:3 daily snapshots, documentary 3:2, and artistic 1:1 square format to perfectly match your creative mood

Plug, Play, Share, Instant OTG Connection

Transfer your stories without the hassle. Connect the C100 directly to your phone via a single Type-C cable to instantly preview, export, and share your raw creations with your inner circle

Limitless Inspiration, Up to 128GB Expanded Storage

Keep your inspiration flowing without limits. With support for up to 128GB of expandable storage, you can snap freely all day long without ever worrying about calculated space.
