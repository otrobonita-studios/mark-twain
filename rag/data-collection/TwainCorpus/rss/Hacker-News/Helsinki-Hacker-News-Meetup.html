Helsinki Hacker News Meetup

This is a coffee-morning meetup for Hacker News posters in Helsinki.

It is completely unofficial - and nothing to at all do with the Y Combinator startup accelerator.

Membership

To keep the group focused on posters (and not just readers!) anyone can join so long as:

  1. they have a non-greenname account
  2. they have 64 karma or more

Often this is waived if you're known personally to someone in the group already.

To join, fill out the Google Form.

Organisation, meeting time, location

We use a WhatsApp group for co-ordination. The time and location of the meetup is announced there.

We meet approximately every 6-8 weeks.

The meetings are usually on a Sunday, 10am-midday, in a coffee shop in central Helsinki - but occasionally we meet for beers too.

The organisers are Cal Paterson and Oleg Podsechin.
