Measured by unobtrusive computer logging, workplace and home. The famous “goldfish attention span” stat is folklore; these are logged durations. The 2025 column marks Mark's January 2025 reaffirmation: the average has held at 47 seconds since ~2016, a steady state. Whisker shows the 40–50s band across the median and independent replications. After a diversion from an active task, re-immersion takes about 25 minutes.

Each switch is cheap for the feed and expensive for the switched. One 47-second loss costs a 25-minute recovery: a 32× asymmetry, and the asymmetry is the business model.
