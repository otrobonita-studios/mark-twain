        Digital Signal Processing Pioneer Bede Liu Dies At 91
    
The IEEE Life Fellow is remembered as a founder of the field
The Liu Family
21h
4 min read
The Liu family includes Bede Liu’s wife, Maria; his daughter, Beatrice; his sister, Jane Jernow; his brother, Hal Liu; and grandchildren Charles and Emily Lovett.
IEEE Life Fellow Bede Liu, a digital signal processing pioneer, died on 7 May. He was 91.
