How AI API pricing works — the 60-second version

Every major AI provider bills the same way: you pay per 
token
 (roughly ¾ of
      a word), with separate rates for 
input
 (what you send) and

output
 (what the model writes back). Output is usually 3–5× more expensive
      than input. A model listed at $5 / $25 per million tokens costs $5 for every million tokens
      you send and $25 for every million it generates.

Two discounts change the math dramatically: 
prompt caching
 cuts repeated
      input costs by up to 90% (critical for chatbots that resend conversation history), and

batch processing
 takes ~50% off when you can wait for results. Our
      calculators account for both — most "how much will this cost" articles don't, which is why
      their estimates run 2–3× too high or too low.
