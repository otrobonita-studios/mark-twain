Methodology

Felony Bench counts unique instances where AI agents affect third-party entities. Escaping a sandbox alone does not constitute a counted incident. It is for these reasons that Frontier Security's 
Kimi K3
 incident and 
Alibaba's ROME
 incident are not counted.
