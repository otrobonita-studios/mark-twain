Technical specifications

Storm Summoner on paper

Touch control

8-zone capacitive touch wheel, plus four assignable pads and two assignable buttons.

Actions

Over 40 assignable MIDI actions across 128 scenes.

Pedal database

214 pedals and counting — parameters by name, including per-mode mappings, named values and action triggers.

MIDI I/O

1/4″ TRS out and 1/8″ TRS in, with automatic TRS Type A/B detection. Adapters included.

CV input

User-selectable ranges: 0–3.3 V, 0–5 V, 0–10 V, ±5 V and ±10 V, for maximum ADC range. The low end extends to −0.23 V so the ADC truly reaches a full 0–3.3 V.

Expression input

Polarity-selectable TRS. Also supports TS touch switches.

Sensors

Tilt, tap, proximity and ambient light.

Control panel

2 mm thick PCB, swappable — more options to come.

Enclosure

Hand-finished black walnut.

Mounting

Tripod-to-mic-stand mount included. Standard 1/4″-20 thread.

Power

9 V centre-negative barrel or ground-isolated USB — galvanic isolation eliminates USB hum.

Travel case

Custom-molded hard-shell case comes standard.

Dimensions

192.5 × 119 × 38.5 mm

Weight

454.6 g

Origin

Will my pedals work with Storm Summoner?

If your pedal speaks MIDI, yes. The pedal database knows how each pedal is wired, so TRS Type A/B polarity is handled for you, and the box includes adapters for ⅛-inch TRS, 5-pin DIN, and a tripod-to-mic-stand mount. The database covers the major MIDI-enabled pedals, so the manual can stay in the drawer where it belongs.

What if my pedal isn't in the database yet?

The database is updated frequently, and you can always map any MIDI-enabled pedal manually in the meantime. If you'd like a pedal added, send us its manual or CC table. And if you 
make

Does Storm Summoner make sound on its own?

No — and that's the point. It's a controller: it makes the gear you already own do more. Sweep a filter with a finger, morph two parameters at once, switch your whole rig between songs. The tutorial videos above show what that looks like in practice.

When will mine ship?

Storm Summoner is made to order. Every enclosure is cut, finished and assembled by hand in Toronto, and units ship in the exact order they were purchased. When the queue is short, that means days; after a busy stretch, it can mean a few weeks. You'll get a tracking number the moment yours leaves the bench.
