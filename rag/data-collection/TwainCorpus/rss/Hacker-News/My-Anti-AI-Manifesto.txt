AI as expression of world

It seems that we have decided that the raw and wild rules of trade market

One of the critical problems of our society is the concentration of
power: for me, it is the root of all evils. Since the ancient history,

political and economical.

The events in the last three centuries

latest expression of such (and very big) concentration. Do you think that

conquering the global market?
