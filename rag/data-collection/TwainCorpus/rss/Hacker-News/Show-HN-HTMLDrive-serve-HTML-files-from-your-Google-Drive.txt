FAQ

Common Questions

Where are my files stored?

Your files always stay in your Google Drive. We store a cached copy of your published content only.

Is this free?

HTML Drive is completely free for up to 10 published pages

Who can view published pages?

Anyone with the public link can view your published pages. No Google account or signup required for viewers.

Can I unpublish a page?

Yes. You can unpublish any page at any time. The public URL will stop working immediately.

What file types are supported?

We support HTML (.html) and Markdown (.md, .markdown) files. Both can be edited and published directly from your Drive.

Is my Google account secure?

We use OAuth 2.0 for secure authentication. We never see your password, and you can revoke access at any time from Google's settings.
