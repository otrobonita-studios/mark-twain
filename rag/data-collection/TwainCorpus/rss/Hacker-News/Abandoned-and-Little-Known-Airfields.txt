“
History
& mystery combined.”

On
the following pages, you will find information on vanished or
abandoned airfields & their unusual histories.

As
a pilot, a particular interest of mine has always been the abandoned
airfields that dot the landscape of much of this country.

Both
for their potential safety value to a pilot in an emergency,

and
also for their sometimes fascinating history, this particular topic
has always held my curiosity.

When
I'm a passenger on commercial flights, I've always found myself
looking out the window, constantly looking for airfields below.

When
I fly as a pilot myself, I've always tried to land at as many
airports as possible,

to
learn a little about each one.
