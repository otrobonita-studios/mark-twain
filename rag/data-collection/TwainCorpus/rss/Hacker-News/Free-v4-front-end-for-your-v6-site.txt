Why is Netiter providing this service for free?

 We believe that when IANA ran out of IPv4 addresses in 2011, the
  internet should already have been converted to dual stack, such
  that new clients and servers could be deployed as IPv6 only
  without any problems. That has not happened.

 We believe that
  owners of IPv6 only sites should not have to pay extra due to
  other parties' failure to upgrade in time. For that reason the
  service is free for website owners. However we will ask
  those internet providers who are sending IPv4 clients to us, to
  pay the cost of their traffic. If you are an internet provider,
  you can 
register
  for a free trial
.
