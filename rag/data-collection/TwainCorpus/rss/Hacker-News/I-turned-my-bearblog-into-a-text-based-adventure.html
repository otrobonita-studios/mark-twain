The Town That Forgot Thursday

Your Aunt Edith has died, or so the solicitor's letter says. She has left you Foxglove Cottage in the seaside town of Wrenfell, along with everything in it — including, the letter notes with some embarrassment, a cat, whereabouts currently unknown.

You have never been to Wrenfell. You cannot find it on any map printed after 1743. The bus driver knew the way, though. Bus drivers always do.

A text adventure in fog and marmalade. Choices are links. Bring a scarf.
