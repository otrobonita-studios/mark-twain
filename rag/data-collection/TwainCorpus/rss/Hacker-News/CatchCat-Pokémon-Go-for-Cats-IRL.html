how it works

From street cat to keepsake in three throws

Frame a cat. Use a snack can.
1

Scan real cats

Open the live camera and frame a real cat: your own, a neighbor's, or one you meet on a walk. On-device detection spots the cat for you.

2

02 · scan

Line up the snack

Drag the snack can, release in the green, and the scan begins. Initial checks run on your phone; verified sightings may sync photos and derived data to our servers — see the Privacy Policy.

3

03 · collect

Sighting logged

The cat joins your album as a keepsake with a generated name, serial number, XP, and coins. Every sighting has a story, and a place in your journal.

Safety first

Map cats are community sightings, not cats physically at that location. CatchCat reminds you to only scan from where you are and stay aware of your surroundings.

privacy

Transparent by design

Local-first detection — with clear server use where needed.
🔒

Local-first, then sync

Cat detection and duplicate checks run on your device where possible. For verified catches, photos, silhouettes, and fingerprint vectors may be stored on our servers and processed by selected providers — see the Privacy Policy.

🛡️

You own your data

CatchCat never sells personal information. Clear privacy controls are built in, with GDPR-friendly consent from the first launch.

🗑️

Easy account deletion

Delete your account anytime from inside the app or via this website. Requests are processed within 30 days.
