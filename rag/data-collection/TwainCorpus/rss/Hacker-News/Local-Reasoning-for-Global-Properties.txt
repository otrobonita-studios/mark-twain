For those of you unfamiliar with Rust, you will be glad to know that the code

Languages of the future

We can now go back to the original topic. What makes programming difficult

This has always been a problem: even the very best human programmers struggle

Ask AI to generate a single function with a well-defined specification and it

Assertions and defensive checks are sometimes conflated, but they are very

good thing. Just as with human written code, many of the defensive checks in AI-generated code are
unnecessary (i.e. they cannot fail).
