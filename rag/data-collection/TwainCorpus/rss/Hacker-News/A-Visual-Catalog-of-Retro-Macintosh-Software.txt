A Visual Catalog of Retro Macintosh Software

A picture might be worth a thousands words, but 
 icons are
a fun way to explore retro apps! Hover over any icon to see the file‘s name, the
country of origin, and the name of the media; then go to 
about page

to find links to the original media at the Macintosh Garden!

Finder application and document icons are highlighted in 
green

and hovering over them will reveal their file type and creator codes.
