Haystack Use Cases

  • Advanced RAG

    Build highly performant RAG pipelines with a multitude of retrieval and generation strategies. From hybrid retrieval to self-correction loops, Haystack has got you covered.

  • AI Agents

    Design production-ready AI agents with standardized tool calling and scalable context engineering. Branching and looping pipelines give you full control over complex, multi-step decision flows.

  • Multimodal AI

    Architect a next generation AI app around all modalities, not just text. Haystack can do tasks like image processing and audio transcription too.
