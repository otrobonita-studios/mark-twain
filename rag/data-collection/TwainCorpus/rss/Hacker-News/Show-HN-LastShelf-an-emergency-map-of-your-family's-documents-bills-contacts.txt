MESSAGE FROM OUR FOUNDER

It's easy to procrastinate about scary topics. I did, until my dad was diagnosed with Stage 3 kidney cancer. Dealing with the emotionally devastating news was hard enough. Asking him to document his will, life insurance, etc...added way more stress before going into surgery. I never want anyone else experience that stress in the middle of a medical emergency. That's why I built LastShelf.

The Problem
 
The hardest moments should not come with a search party.

When someone has a medical emergency or dies, families are suddenly asked to find documents, call the right people, access accounts, understand insurance, and keep important bills paid.

LastShelf helps you do the loving thing ahead of time: leave them a clear path.

The gap
 
Leave clarity, not questions.
