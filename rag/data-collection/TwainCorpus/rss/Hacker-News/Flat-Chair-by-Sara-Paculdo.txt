Flat Chair by Sara Paculdo

August 7th, 2026 | 
Tech
 | 

Innovative 
chair
 with flat-pack design created around the principles of efficient manufacturing, compact transportation, and structural simplicity.

Made from durable fiberglass panels, the 
chair
 is delivered as flat-packed product and assembled by the user in a matter of minutes.

Flat Chair designed by 
Sara Paculdo
 greatly reduces 
shipping
 volume, storage requirements, and transportation costs.

Flat Chair relies on curves, folds, and interlocking components that form strength through tension rather than excessive material use.

By arriving flat and assembling later, design addresses logistical inefficiencies.

The Flat Chair Project is a wonderful example of doing more with less.
