Each of these hotels provides a great mix of accessibility to Disrupt, and plenty of options for catering your San Francisco experience to your tastes. 

The Hyatt Regency 

Located right along the waterfront, the Hyatt Regency places you just a brief walk away from Disrupt and its many side events, and an even shorter distance from the city’s beloved Ferry Building. Hog Island Oyster Company or Gott’s Roadside make for great dining options, and if you’re extending your stay, you can take the ferry to scenic spots like Sausalito and Angel Island. 

Hotel Zelos 

For those with energy to spare after an exciting day at Disrupt, Hotel Zelos is a four-star hotel with a rooftop bar perfect for post-event conversations and meetups. Or for the urban explorers out there, it’s much easier to hop on public transportation if you want to have your post-Disrupt networking elsewhere in the city. 

InterContinental San Francisco 

You can’t get any closer than the InterContinental, which sits right beside Moscone West and is the perfect home base for exploring the event and branching out into everything else. Within walking distance is the world-class San Francisco Museum of Modern Art and the Metreon theater — though we can’t promise its 70MM IMAX will still be playing the Odyssey… 

The Grand Hyatt San Francisco 

The Grand Hyatt towers over San Francisco, perched beside Union Square, where you can check out some of the city’s best restaurants or best shopping in your downtime. Explore old-school steak houses, new spins on Vietnamese cuisine, or one of the city’s most celebrated cocktail bars. Plus, it’s a quick walk from historic Chinatown and its many attractions.
