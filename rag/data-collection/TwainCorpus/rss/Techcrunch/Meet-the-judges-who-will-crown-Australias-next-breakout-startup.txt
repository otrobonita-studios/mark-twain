TechCrunch Startup Battlefield is coming to Australia — and we’re 
partnering with Stripe
 to find the country’s most exciting early-stage startups. On August 19, eight Australian startups will take the stage at 
Stripe
 Tour Sydney
 to pitch live in front of investors, press, and the tech community. Three will win prizes. One will earn an automatic ticket to 
TechCrunch Disrupt 2026
, taking place in San Francisco from October 13-15.

The secret to finding the next breakout founder? The judges.

Startup Battlefield has a track record that speaks for itself. The competition has launched over 1,700 companies globally, including Dropbox, Cloudflare, Discord, and Trello. Alumni have collectively raised $32 billion and produced 250+ exits. The last time Startup Battlefield came to Sydney, the winner was HealthMatch — now valued at over $25 million with a million patients globally.

That success doesn’t happen by accident. It comes from having the right people in the room, the ones who can spot potential, ask the hard questions, and open doors for founders. This year, we’ve assembled three judges who represent the best of Australia’s startup ecosystem and global fintech innovation.

What’s on the line

The grand prize winner takes home $15,000 in Stripe fee credits — real money that can fuel growth — plus automatic entry into Startup Battlefield 200 at 
TechCrunch Disrupt 2026
. That’s not just a prize. That’s a launchpad. Second place takes $5,000 in Stripe credits. Third place takes $2,000 in Stripe credits. But more importantly, all finalists get in the room with these judges, get press attention, and get access to the 
Stripe Tour Sydney
 audience of founders, operators, and investors.
