AI researchers continue to leave Google for its rivals

Top AI researchers Jonas Adler and Alexander Pritzel are leaving Google for Anthropic, according to 
Bloomberg
. Per the report, Adler and Pritzel played key roles in the development of Google’s Gemini model.

TechCrunch reached out to Google for comment.

These departures are part of a concerning trend for Google. Last week, legendary AI researcher 
Noam Shazeer
 
announced
 that he was 
leaving Google
 for OpenAI. Shazeer had been at Google since 2000, save for the three years he spent building his 
controversial
 chatbot startup, Character.AI (which Google effectively acqui-hired for $2.7 billion, in part to bring Shazeer back to work on Gemini).

Just days after Shazeer made his announcement, Google DeepMind director 
John Jumper
 said he was leaving Google for Anthropic. Alongside DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis, Jumper won the 2024 
Nobel Prize in Chemistry
 for his work on AlphaFold, which can predict 3D protein structures from animo acid sequences.

As OpenAI and Anthropic 
prepare to go public
, this trend could continue — it’s a great time for the companies to recruit top AI talent with a promise of equity.
